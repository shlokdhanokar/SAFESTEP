🦯 SafeStep

Obstacle vision · live demo

Point your camera at a room. SafeStep finds obstacles, works out how far away and in which direction they are, and warns you with a tone, a spoken alert and a simulated vibration pattern.

📷
CameraYour browser will ask for permission. Video is processed on your device and never uploaded — there is no server to upload it to.
🔊
AudioWarning tones and speech, generated locally. Your microphone is never accessed.
📦
~24 MB downloadThe YOLOv8 model and the inference runtime load once, then are cached by your browser.
Not a mobility device. This is an experimental demo. It misses obstacles — glass, kerbs, drop-offs and anything at head height. Never use it in place of a cane, guide dog or your own judgement.
